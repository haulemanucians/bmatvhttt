Governance

Governance defines policies, roles, and frameworks for security management.

  • Develop Security Policies
  • Assign Roles & Responsibilities
  • Assess and Manage Risks

Compliance

Compliance ensures that security policies follow laws, standards, and regulations.

  • Follow ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA
  • Conduct Regular Audits
  • Provide Proof of Adherence
